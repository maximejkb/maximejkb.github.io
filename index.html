Maxime Kawawa-Beaudan (b. 1999, New York, NY) is a writer, painter, and researcher. His fiction has appeared in Glimmer Train, Expat Press, and the YoungArts Writer’s Anthology. He won Glimmer Train’s Short Story Award for New Writers, was a National YoungArts Foundation finalist in writing, and has been recognized by New Millennium Writings. He lives in Brooklyn. In his free time he enjoys riding motorcycles, taking photos of friends, and befriending stray animals.
